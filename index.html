How it works

Three steps to a basket

0xaD3424B51b9686314D88D6Cb052b543E755afbCc

Step 1 - Gather

Pick three or more assets

Any mix of Robinhood Chain coins, tokenized stocks like tTSLA and tNVDA, or tokenized real-world assets. Paste a contract address or start from a pre-built basket.

Step 2 - Weight

Set the weights and name it

Set each asset's allocation and give the basket its own name. The weights are enforced on-chain, in the open, and anyone can verify them.

Step 3 - Trade

It trades as one

Your basket lists instantly. Buyers hold a single share token that tracks the whole bundle, and can go long, short, or trade perps on it up to 5x.

A basket is an on-chain ETF: three or more tokens with fixed weights, wrapped so it trades as one asset. Buying a share gives you exposure to every asset in the basket at its set weight.

Anyone with a connected wallet. There is no listing committee and no approval queue. You pick the assets, set the weights, and the protocol mints it.

Robinhood Chain, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and Solana. A single basket can hold assets that live on different chains, and deploys run through one launch flow.

Yes. Every basket has a perps market. You can go long or short with up to 5x leverage, and positions are collateralised from your custodial balance.

Robinhood Chain native coins, tokenized stocks such as tTSLA, tNVDA and tAAPL, tokenized real-world assets like tGOLD, and any token you can paste a contract address for.
