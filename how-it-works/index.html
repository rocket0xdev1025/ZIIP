The protocol

How a basket is made

No listing committee. Gather the assets, set the weights, and the protocol mints the shares. Three steps and your basket trades like any other asset on the chain.

Create a basketBrowse existing baskets
Step 1 - Gather

Pick three or more assets

Any mix of Robinhood Chain coins, tokenized stocks like tTSLA and tNVDA, or tokenized real-world assets. Paste a contract address or start from a pre-built basket.

Step 2 - Weight

Set the weights and name it

Set each asset's allocation and give the basket its own name. The weights are enforced on-chain, in the open, and anyone can verify them.

Step 3 - Trade

It trades as one

Your basket lists instantly. Buyers hold a single share token that tracks the whole bundle, and can go long, short, or trade perps on it up to 5x.

What that means in practice

Anything you can address, you can basket

Robinhood Chain native coins, tokenized equities like tTSLA and tNVDA, tokenized commodities like tGOLD, and any token you can paste a contract address for. Assets can sit on different chains inside the same basket.

Weights are enforced, not suggested

The allocation you publish is stored with the basket and applied on every buy and sell. Anyone can read it. There is no rebalancing desk quietly changing what you bought.

One share token, one price

Holders get a single token that tracks the whole basket. It moves as one line in a wallet instead of a dozen positions to manage by hand.

Long, short or perps up to 5x

Every basket gets a perps market from the moment it lists. Collateral comes from your custodial balance, and liquidation prices are shown before you open a position.
